
#AI Resume Tailor & ATS Optimizer

What it does:
<br>
<br>
PDF Parsing: Upload your resume as a PDF and it automatically reads through the text.
<br>
AI Analysis: Uses `gemini-2.5-flash` to evaluate how well your resume matches the job posting.
<br>
Smart Feedback: Gives you an estimated compatibility score (0-100), points out missing keywords, and suggests ways to rewrite your bullet points.


Tech Stack
<br>
<br>
Python 
<br>
Streamlit (for the UI)
<br>
Google GenAI SDK** (Gemini API)
<br>
pypdf (for reading PDFs)
